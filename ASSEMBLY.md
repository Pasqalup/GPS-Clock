# Building & Assembly
## Wire Bending
1. Print out the [PDF assembly templates](./gps%20clock%20wiring%20template.pdf) and use them as a guide for bending the wires.
2. Straighten out 20AWG brass wire
3. Bend the wires according to the templates. You can use pliers to help with bending
4. Cut off excess wire
